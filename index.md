## 2. Information We Collect

Krish Ludo provides offline and online multiplayer gaming experiences.

### Offline Mode

Offline Pass & Play mode does not require users to register an account or provide personal information to play.

### Online Multiplayer Mode

To enable online multiplayer functionality, the application uses Google Firebase services.

Information processed may include:

- Anonymous Firebase user identifiers.
- Multiplayer room codes.
- Player display names, if provided.
- Game progress and multiplayer session information.
- Technical information processed by Firebase, such as IP addresses and service-related diagnostics.

Users are not required to provide their real name, phone number, or email address to access anonymous multiplayer functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used for the following purposes:

- Providing online multiplayer functionality.
- Creating and managing private game rooms.
- Synchronizing gameplay between connected players.
- Maintaining application functionality and security.
- Identifying and resolving technical issues.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Krish Ludo uses the following third-party services:

- Firebase Authentication.
- Firebase Realtime Database.

These services are provided by Google and may process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Multiplayer information necessary for gameplay may be visible to other participants in the same game room.

## 5. Advertising

The current version of Krish Ludo does not display advertisements.

We do not sell personal information or use gameplay information for targeted advertising.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Online multiplayer information may be stored using Google Firebase infrastructure.

We aim to use appropriate security measures to protect information against unauthorized access, modification, and disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Multiplayer information is retained according to the application's operational requirements and configured Firebase data retention practices.

Users may contact the developer to request deletion of information associated with their use of the application.

Information required for security or legal obligations may be retained where necessary.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Krish Ludo is a board game application. Users who are children should use the application with appropriate parental or guardian supervision.

We do not intentionally request children's real names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other direct contact information through the game's anonymous multiplayer functionality.

Parents or guardians may contact us regarding privacy concerns or requests to delete their child's information.
